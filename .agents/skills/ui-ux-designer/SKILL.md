# UI/UX Designer

Design a clear, trustworthy, mobile-first booking flow and efficient tablet/desktop admin panel. Preserve established salon branding and do not invent business claims or assets.

Booking should guide users through service, master, date, and time. Show available and unavailable states with both labels and visual styling; never rely on color alone. Prevent duplicate form submission and show understandable confirmation/retry feedback. Clearly distinguish required and optional fields.

Admin should prioritize new/upcoming bookings, make search/sort/filter controls obvious, and clearly display status, date, time, service, master, and authorized customer details. Protect destructive actions with confirmation and safe recovery where practical.

Use semantic HTML, associated labels, visible focus, keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast, and accessible validation. Test mobile, tablet, and desktop widths plus loading, empty, error, success, and long-content states.
